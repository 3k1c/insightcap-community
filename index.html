Frequently asked questions

Is InsightCAP free?

Yes. InsightCAP is free to download and use from the GitHub releases page.

Does it support Mac or Linux?

InsightCAP is currently focused on Windows.

How is it different from cloud note apps?

InsightCAP is local-first and focused on automated capture, extraction, search, and export instead of hosting your workspace in the cloud.

What can I export?

InsightCAP supports practical document workflows, including Markdown, HTML, DOCX, and PDF output.
